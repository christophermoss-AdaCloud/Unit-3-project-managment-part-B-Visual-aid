About This Glossary

This glossary contains simple definitions of important project management terms. Use it whenever you come across a word you don't understand!

Key Terms

Budget
The amount of money available to spend on a project. Example: "We have a budget of £5,000 for the school event."
Deadline
The date or time by which something must be finished. Example: "The deadline for the website is 30th June."
Deliverable
Something that must be produced and given to the client or stakeholder. Example: "The deliverables are a mobile app and user manual."
Dependency
When one task can't start until another task is finished. Example: "Testing has a dependency on coding - you can't test until the code is written."
Gantt Chart
A visual timeline showing all project tasks, when they start, and when they end. It helps you see the whole project at a glance.
Milestone
An important point or achievement in a project. Example: "Completing the design is our first milestone."
Project Brief
A document that explains what the project is about, including goals, timeline, budget, and team members.
Project Manager
The person in charge of planning and running a project. They make sure tasks are done on time and the team works together well.
Project Scope
What is and isn't included in the project. Example: "The scope includes a website but not a mobile app."
Resource
Anything needed to complete a project: people, money, equipment, or time. Example: "Our main resources are 5 team members and £10,000."
Risk
Something that could go wrong in a project. Example: "A risk is that a key team member might leave."
Stakeholder
A person or group who is affected by or interested in the project. Example: "The stakeholders are students, teachers, and parents."
Task
A specific piece of work that needs to be done as part of the project. Example: "Design the logo" is a task.
Team Member
Someone who works on the project and has specific responsibilities.
Timeline
The schedule showing when things will happen in the project, from start to finish.
Quality Assurance (QA)
Checking that work meets the required standards. Example: "QA testing checks the app works properly."
Objective
A specific goal you want to achieve. Example: "Our objective is to increase website visitors by 50%."
Priority
How important or urgent something is. High priority means it's very important.
Success Criteria
The standards or conditions that show a project has succeeded. Example: "Success criteria: app works on all phones, 100+ downloads in first week."
Work Package
A group of related tasks that one person or team is responsible for. Example: "The design work package includes logo, colors, and layout."
