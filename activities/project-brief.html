💡 Tips for a Great Project Brief

  • Be Specific: Don't just say "plan event" - say what type of event and when
  • Be Realistic: Make sure your goals match your time and budget
  • Think Ahead: Consider what could go wrong and how to prevent it
  • Be Clear: Anyone reading your brief should understand the project
