What You'll Do

Match team members to the right roles based on their skills and experience. Choose wisely - the right person in the right role is key to success!

📖 Instructions

💡 Tips for Assigning Roles

  • Match Skills: Put people in roles that use their strengths
  • Consider Experience: Someone who's led projects before is good for Project Manager
  • Think About Personality: Detail-oriented people are great testers
  • Communication Matters: The Project Manager needs to be a good communicator
