Think About What You've Learned

Reflection helps you understand what you've learned and how you can use it. Take your time with these questions!

Reflection Questions

Answer these questions about your learning journey. You can write your answers here or discuss them with your teacher or classmates.

1. What I Learned

What are the three most important things you learned about project management?

2. What Surprised Me

Was there anything that surprised you or that you didn't expect to learn?

3. Real-Life Connections

Think of a real project (at school, home, or in your community). How could you use project management skills for it?

4. Skills I Developed

What new skills did you develop? (e.g., planning, organizing, problem-solving)

5. What I Found Challenging

What parts of project management did you find difficult? How did you overcome these challenges?

6. My Favorite Activity

Which activity did you enjoy the most? Why?

7. Future Goals

How will you use what you've learned in the future?

💡 Tips for Good Reflection

  • Be Honest: There are no wrong answers - just share your real thoughts
  • Be Specific: Give examples when you can
  • Think Deeply: Don't rush - take time to really think about each question
  • Look Forward: Think about how you'll use what you've learned
