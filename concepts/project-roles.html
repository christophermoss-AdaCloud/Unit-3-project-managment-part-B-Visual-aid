Working as a Team

Just like a football team, every person on a project team has a specific role and responsibilities. Everyone needs to work together to succeed!

Key Project Roles

🎯 Project Manager

What they do: The project manager is like the team captain. They make sure everyone knows what to do and keeps the project on track.

Responsibilities:
  • Create the project plan
  • Assign tasks to team members
  • Monitor progress
  • Solve problems that come up
  • Communicate with the client
  • Make sure deadlines are met
Think of them as: The conductor of an orchestra - they don't play every instrument, but they make sure everyone plays together in harmony!

👔 Project Sponsor

What they do: The sponsor is usually a senior person in the company who provides the money and support for the project.

Responsibilities:
  • Approve the budget
  • Make big decisions
  • Remove obstacles
  • Support the project manager
Think of them as: The team owner who provides resources and makes final decisions!

💼 Team Members

What they do: These are the people who actually do the work. They each have specific skills and tasks.

Different Types of Team Members:
  • Designers: Create how things look
  • Developers: Build the product
  • Testers: Check everything works properly
  • Researchers: Find out information
  • Writers: Create content and documentation
Think of them as: The players on a sports team - each has their position and skills!

🙋 Stakeholders

What they do: These are people who are affected by the project or have an interest in it, but don't work on it day-to-day.

Examples of Stakeholders:
  • Customers who will use the product
  • Company directors
  • Other departments in the company
  • Investors
Think of them as: The fans - they care about the outcome even though they're not playing!

How Roles Work Together - An Example

Project: Creating a new school website

Project Sponsor (Headteacher): Provides £5,000 budget and approves the design

Project Manager (IT Coordinator): Plans the timeline, assigns tasks, checks progress

Team Members:

  • Designer creates the look of the website
  • Developer builds the website
  • Teacher writes the content
  • Student tests the website

Stakeholders: Teachers, students, parents who will use the website

Why Different Roles Matter

  • Clear Responsibilities: Everyone knows exactly what they should be doing
  • Right Skills: Each person can focus on what they're good at
  • Better Communication: People know who to talk to about different things
  • Accountability: Each person is responsible for their part
  • Efficiency: Work gets done faster when everyone has a clear role

🤔 Quick Activity

Think about a group project you've done. Can you identify:

  1. Who was the project manager (leader)?
  2. Who were the team members and what did each person do?
  3. Who was the sponsor (teacher who approved it)?
  4. Who were the stakeholders (people who would see or use the result)?
