The Big Idea

Planning is like creating a map before you start a journey. It shows you where you're going and helps you get there safely and on time.

What Happens Without Planning? 😰

Imagine building a house without a plan...

  • ❌ You might forget to include stairs to the second floor!
  • ❌ Windows and doors might not fit properly
  • ❌ You could run out of money halfway through
  • ❌ It might take twice as long as it should
  • ❌ The house might not be safe to live in

This is why planning is SO important!

Benefits of Good Planning

⏰ Saves Time

When you know what to do and when to do it, you work faster and don't waste time figuring things out.

Example: A planned project takes 3 months. Without planning, it might take 6 months!

💰 Saves Money

Planning helps you know exactly what you need to buy and when. No expensive surprises!

Example: You order materials at the right time, not when you're desperate and prices are high.

✅ Better Quality

When you plan, you have time to do things properly, not rush at the last minute.

Example: You test your work properly because you planned time for it.

😊 Less Stress

Everyone knows what they're doing and when. No panic or confusion!

Example: Team members feel confident because they know the plan.

The Planning Process - Step by Step

  1. Define the Goal: What exactly are you trying to achieve?
    Example: "Create a mobile app for ordering food"
  2. Break it into Tasks: What smaller jobs need to be done?
    Example: Design interface, write code, test app, launch app
  3. Put Tasks in Order: What needs to be done first?
    Example: You must design before you code, code before you test
  4. Estimate Time: How long will each task take?
    Example: Design = 2 weeks, Coding = 4 weeks, Testing = 1 week
  5. Assign People: Who will do each task?
    Example: Designer does design, Developer does coding
  6. Calculate Costs: How much will it cost?
    Example: Salaries + software licenses + marketing

The 5 Ws of Planning

WHO - Who will do the work?

WHAT - What needs to be done?

WHEN - When does each task need to be completed?

WHERE - Where will the work happen?

WHY - Why is this project important?

Planning Tools We Use

Project managers use special tools to help them plan. You'll learn about these in the next section:

  • Gantt Charts: Show what happens when
  • Mind Maps: Organize ideas visually
  • To-Do Lists: Track tasks that need doing
  • Calendars: Show important dates
