Simple Definition

Project Management is the way we organize and control work to complete a specific goal on time and within budget.

Think of it Like This... 🎂

Imagine you're baking a cake for a birthday party:

  • The Goal: Have a delicious cake ready for the party
  • The Time Limit: The party starts at 3pm
  • The Budget: You have £20 to buy ingredients
  • The Plan: You need to buy ingredients, mix them, bake, and decorate
  • The Team: Maybe you ask someone to help decorate

That's project management! You're managing resources (money, time, people) to achieve your goal.

Key Parts of Project Management

🎯 Goal (What)

What do you want to achieve?

Example: Build a website for a local shop

⏰ Time (When)

When does it need to be finished?

Example: Website must be ready in 2 months

💰 Budget (How Much)

How much money can you spend?

Example: We have £5,000 to spend

👥 Team (Who)

Who will do the work?

Example: 1 designer, 2 developers, 1 tester

Why Do We Need Project Management?

  1. Avoid Chaos: Without a plan, people don't know what to do
  2. Save Money: Good planning stops you wasting money on mistakes
  3. Meet Deadlines: Keeps everyone on track to finish on time
  4. Better Quality: When you plan properly, you do better work
  5. Happy Customers: Delivering what you promised makes people happy

Real World Examples 🌍

Small Projects:

  • Planning a school event
  • Creating a poster for a campaign
  • Building a simple app

Big Projects:

  • Building a new hospital
  • Launching a new product
  • Organizing the Olympics

All use the same basic project management ideas!
